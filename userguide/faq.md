# FAQ

## General Questions

### What is this project?

It is a role-based classroom management and learning platform for teachers, students, and administrators.

### Who should use it?

Teachers can use it to organize classes and coursework. Students can use it to access classes, assignments, resources, and progress information.

### Do I need a separate account for each role?

Yes. Your experience depends on the role attached to your account. The system routes users to different dashboards after login.

### I registered as the wrong role. What should I do?

Create a new account with the correct role, or ask the project team to update the role in the underlying user record.

## Teacher Questions

### Where do I create a class?

Start in `My Classes`. Class-related management and class detail views are available there.

### Where do I create lesson plans?

Use the `Lesson Plans` section from the teacher sidebar.

### How do I create assignments?

Open `Assignments`, choose the create option, select the class, and fill in the assignment details.

### Can I take attendance?

Yes. Use the `Attendance` page to select a class, mark attendance, and save changes.

### Where do I send announcements or messages?

Use `Announcements` for class-wide posts and `Messages` for direct communication.

## Student Questions

### How do I join a class?

Open `Available Classes`, choose a class, and enroll if it is open.

### Where can I see my assignments?

Use the `Assignments` page from the student sidebar.

### Where do I find class resources?

Use `Resources` or open one of your enrolled classes and review its materials there.

### How do I check grades and progress?

Use `Grades`, `Analytics`, and `Progress`.
